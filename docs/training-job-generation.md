# Phase 11 LlamaFactory job generation

Phase 11 creates a reviewable external training-job bundle; it does not execute training. Every job is department-scoped and starts from one exact succeeded, approved, unpurged Phase 10 dataset. `system_admin` and `department_admin` may enqueue, cancel, and review jobs only within their department. `system_admin`, `department_admin`, and `instructor` may read safe metadata. There is no cross-department bypass and no artifact, dataset, configuration, download, or raw-argument endpoint.

The fixed contract is LlamaFactory `0.9.5` with `Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B` at immutable revision `c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca`, Apache-2.0. The fixed profiles are `phase11-qwen3-0.6b-lora-v1` and `phase11-qwen3-0.6b-qlora-nf4-v1`. Both use SFT, `qwen3_nothink`, disabled thinking, a 8,192-token cutoff, deterministic seeds, and no remote code. QLoRA adds only reviewed bitsandbytes NF4 four-bit settings. The normal worker never downloads or loads model/tokenizer bytes and CI does not install LlamaFactory. A compatibility smoke check is explicitly opt-in through `DEPTSLM_RUN_LLAMFACTORY_CONFIG_SMOKE=1` in a separately provisioned environment.

The immutable bundle is `manifest.json`, `training.yaml`, `dataset_info.json`, `train.jsonl`, and `validation.jsonl`. Train and validation records retain their Phase 10 byte representation after strict validation. Files are written only to private descriptor-relative staging, atomically renamed, and rehashed before PostgreSQL receives content-free digests, counts, contracts, lifecycle state, and review metadata. PostgreSQL and external storage are not transactional; only a committed succeeded job is authority for review. Reconciliation and retention purge are explicit, bounded, department-scoped operations. They clean only exact UUID attempt paths and never claim deletion from backups or audit history.

No question, instruction, answer, provenance, filename, path, model output, vector, training log, model weight, adapter, token, or credential is persisted in PostgreSQL, public responses, audit events, logs, or the browser. Phase 11 does not install or invoke LlamaFactory, run a scheduler, create training logs, produce adapters, promote a model, or implement Phase 12.
